Create a 10-second vertical 9:16 animated sports short. Start exactly from the uploaded frame: the same golden-tan capybara in teal bowling jersey and cream trousers, the same yellow-headed lime-green budgerigar, the same glossy blue bowling ball and warm bowling alley. Keep the charming tactile 3D animation look and both characters recognizable.

ACTION TIMING
0–1s: The capybara focuses down the lane and shifts its weight. The little budgerigar does one eager hop on the ball return, cheering its friend.
1–5s: In a readable full-body shot the capybara makes a smooth controlled four-step professional bowling approach. The blue ball drops into a pendulum swing in the right paw, travels into a high backswing, then forward. Finish with a bent left knee and sliding left foot; the right leg crosses behind for balance and the free left arm opens sideways. Release the ball close to the wooden floor, smoothly rolling forward, with the right arm following through upward.
5–8s: Stay on the same axis facing the pins. The released blue ball rolls away along the lane with subtle spin and a gentle hook toward the headpin pocket. The distant white pins scatter naturally when the ball hits them. The bird follows the rolling ball with its gaze and excited wing flutters.
8–10s: The capybara rises from the finishing pose, turns partly back toward its little friend with a proud joyful smile and raises one paw. The bird remains near the ball return, hopping and chirping happily. End while the friends are beginning to celebrate, leaving their bodies visible for a continuation.

CAMERA
One continuous sports-film shot, no cuts. Begin at the rear three-quarter angle of the frame. Gently track forward with the approach while keeping the capybara's entire body and bowling arm visible, then ease back just enough for the bird and capybara to share the ending frame. Preserve lane direction and physical contact with the floor.

AUDIO
Bowling alley room tone, soft shoe slide, rolling ball, a crisp satisfying pin crash at impact, cheerful natural budgie chirps. Light playful rhythmic instrumental under the action, quieter than the pin crash. No speech.
Keep bowling believable, body motion fluid and weighted. One ball is released once; it rolls rather than flying. Keep characters, outfits and props consistent. No text, no title, no logos.
