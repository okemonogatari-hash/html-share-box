A delightful 10-second cinematic bowling short starring this capybara and its yellow-green budgie friend. Warm polished 3D animation, professional sports camera work. The capybara settles into a focused stance, then performs a brisk, fluent four-step approach with a high pendulum backswing. At about 5.2 seconds the final slide and forward swing meet: the blue ball leaves the right paw just above the lane and rolls smoothly along the wood. Cut to a low tracking view following the spinning blue ball for about 2.8 seconds. It reaches the front pin at about 8 seconds; that contact starts an energetic, believable chain reaction through the rack. Cut back to the capybara smiling as the little budgie flutters happily beside it. Make the throw athletic and the friendship playful. Sound: shoes on the approach, a rolling rumble, then the sharp pin crash at contact, happy bird chirps.
